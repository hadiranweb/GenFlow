# ADR 004: Work-Style & Personality Over "Culture Fit"

## Status
Accepted

## Context
"Culture fit" is a popular recruitment metric but is highly subjective, prone to unconscious bias, and frequently leads to homogeneous teams lacking cognitive diversity.

## Decision
We replace the concept of "Culture Fit" with highly scientific, objective **Work-Style and Personality Axis Dimensions**, modeled on the **Big Five** and **RIASEC** psychometric frameworks. Rather than matching for "clones," we match candidate work-styles to the operational environment and team requirements.

## Consequences
* Eradication of subjective bias.
* Diversified team profiles matching distinct business needs.
* Highly measurable, comparable candidate-to-position axes.
